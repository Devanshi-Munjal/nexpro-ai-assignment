Dunder Mifflin Paper Company

Business Expense Policy

1. General Rules

Business expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and related to legitimate company activities.

Employees should obtain the required approval before making unusual or high-value business purchases.

2. Business Meals

Employees travelling for business may claim reasonable meal expenses.

The maximum reimbursable amount for an individual business meal is ₹1,500 including taxes.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable.

3. Equipment

Employees may request reimbursement for approved work-related equipment.

Equipment purchases above ₹15,000 require department-head approval before purchase.

4. Software

Software subscriptions required for an employee's work must be approved by the employee's manager and the IT department before purchase.

Personal software subscriptions are not reimbursable.

5. Receipts

Receipts are required for expenses of ₹500 or more.

If a receipt cannot be obtained, the employee must provide a written explanation when submitting the expense claim.

6. Expense Submission

Expense claims should normally be submitted within 15 calendar days of the expense being incurred.

Finance may request additional documentation before approving a claim.

7. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Finance team. Approval should be obtained before the expense is incurred whenever practical.
