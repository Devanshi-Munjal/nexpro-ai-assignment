# Dunder Mifflin Paper Company
# Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Leave

Employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave should normally be requested at least five working days before the planned absence. Unused annual leave may be carried forward into the following calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

Carried-forward annual leave must be used by March 31 of the following year. Unused carried-forward days expire after March 31.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave may be taken without advance notice when an employee is unexpectedly ill. Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably  possible.
A medical certificate may be requested when an employee is absent for more than 3 consecutive working days.

## 3. Public Holidays

Dunder Mifflin observes the public holidays listed on the annual company holiday calendar. Public holidays do not reduce an employee's annual leave balance.

## 4. Leave Approval

Leave requests must be submitted through the employee portal. Manager approval is required before planned annual leave is considered
confirmed. 
Employees should not assume that submitting a request automatically means
the leave has been approved.

## 5. Parental Leave

Eligible employees may take 16 weeks of paid parental leave following the
birth or adoption of a child. Additional unpaid leave may be available subject to applicable law and company approval.
