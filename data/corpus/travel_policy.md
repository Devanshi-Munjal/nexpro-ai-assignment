# Dunder Mifflin Paper Company
# Business Travel Policy

## 1. Travel Approval

Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim reimbursement for eligible travel expenses.
Business travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager before booking.
International business travel additionally requires approval from the employee's department head and the Finance team.

## 2. Domestic Air Travel

For domestic business trips, employees may book economy-class air travel.
Flights should normally be booked at least seven days before departure when
practical.

## 3. Domestic Rail Travel

Employees travelling domestically by rail may book AC chair car or executive-class rail travel when appropriate for the trip.

## 4. Hotel Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to ₹6,000 per night for travel to Tier 1 cities.

Hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to ₹4,000 per night for travel to other cities.

Employees should use company-approved hotels or booking channels when available.

## 5. International Travel

Employees travelling internationally may book economy-class air travel. Premium economy may be approved for journeys longer than eight hours. 
International tickets must not be purchased before the required department head and Finance approvals have been obtained.

## 6. Local Transportation

Reasonable expenses for taxis, app-based transportation, airport transfers, and public transportation used for approved business travel are reimbursable.
Receipts are required for individual transportation expenses above ₹500.

## 7. Travel Advance

Employees may request a travel advance up to seven calendar days before the planned departure date.

Travel advances must be settled within 10 business days after returning from
the trip.

## 8. Travel Reimbursement

Travel reimbursement claims should normally be submitted through the expense system within 15 calendar days after returning from a business trip.
Expenses that were not approved in advance may be rejected unless the employee can demonstrate that the expense was necessary and urgent.
