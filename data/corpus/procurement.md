# Dunder Mifflin Paper Company
# Procurement Policy

## 1. Standard Purchases

Employees should use the company's approved procurement system when
purchasing office supplies, equipment, software, or other goods for business use.

Employees should not use personal funds for recurring business purchases
unless the purchase has been approved under the applicable expense process.

## 2. Office Supplies

Routine office supplies such as paper, pens, folders, printer supplies, and other standard stationery should be requested through the office procurement system.

Employees should contact the office administrator when a standard supply is not available through the normal ordering process.

## 3. Equipment Purchases

Equipment required for an employee's work may be requested through the
procurement system.

Equipment purchases above ₹15,000 require department-head approval before the purchase is made.

Equipment purchases of ₹15,000 or less require manager approval.

## 4. Software Purchases

Software required for an employee's work must be approved by the employee's manager and the IT department before purchase.

Employees must not purchase software using a personal account when the
software will be used to store or process company information.

## 5. Vendor Selection

For purchases involving a new vendor, Procurement may request quotations or additional vendor information before approving the purchase.

Employees must disclose any potential conflict of interest involving a
vendor.

## 6. Confidential Information

Employees must not provide confidential customer, employee, financial, or
pricing information to a vendor unless the vendor has been authorized to
receive that information.

## 7. Purchase Approval

Approval must be obtained before committing the company to a purchase.

An employee should not assume that submitting a purchase request means the purchase has been approved.

## 8. Urgent Purchases

Urgent purchases may follow an expedited approval process when delaying the purchase would materially affect business operations.

The employee must document the reason for the urgent purchase.

## 9. Procurement Questions

Questions about vendors, purchasing procedures, or approval requirements
should be directed to the Procurement team.
