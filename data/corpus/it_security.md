# Dunder Mifflin Paper Company
# IT Security Policy

## 1. Passwords

Employees must use a unique password for their company account. Employees must not share passwords with colleagues, managers, vendors,
support personnel, or any other person.

Company passwords must contain at least 12 characters and should use a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols.

## 2. Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for company email, VPN access, and other systems designated by the IT department.

Employees must never approve an MFA request they did not initiate. Unexpected MFA requests should be reported to IT Security.

## 3. Company Devices

Company laptops must use the security software and disk encryption configured
by IT. Employees must not disable endpoint protection without written approval from
IT Security.

## 4. Lost or Stolen Devices

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to IT Security within
4 hours of discovery.
Employees should also notify their manager when a company device is lost or
stolen.

## 5. Data Handling

Confidential company information must only be stored in approved company
systems.
Employees must not upload confidential documents to personal cloud-storage
accounts, personal email accounts, or unapproved AI tools.

## 6. Phishing

Suspicious emails should be reported using the company's phishing-reporting
button.
Employees should not click links or open attachments in messages they suspect
may be malicious.

## 7. Remote Access

Employees accessing internal systems from outside the office must use the
company-approved VPN where VPN access is required.
VPN credentials must never be shared with another person.
