Dunder Mifflin Paper Company

Remote Work Policy

1. Eligibility

Employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week after completing their probation period.

Remote work is subject to manager approval and business requirements.

2. Working Hours

Employees working remotely must remain available during the company's core working hours of 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees may adjust their start and end times with prior manager approval, provided they complete their required working hours.

3. Office Attendance

Employees may be required to attend the office for team meetings, collaboration sessions, training, or other business needs.

When an employee is asked to attend the office on a scheduled remote-work day, the manager should provide reasonable advance notice whenever practical.

4. Equipment

The company provides eligible remote employees with a laptop and required security software.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable workspace and reliable internet connection.

5. Security

Company data must only be accessed through approved company devices and services.

Employees must not store confidential company information on personal cloud-storage accounts.

Employees working remotely must use the company's approved VPN when accessing internal systems that require VPN access.
